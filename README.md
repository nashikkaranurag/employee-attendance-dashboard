# Employee Attendance & Presence Analytics

## Problem Statement
HR teams in mid-to-large organisations often manage 
attendance through manual records or disconnected 
spreadsheets, making it difficult to spot patterns 
or make data-driven workplace decisions. This project 
analysed employee attendance records to identify work 
preference trends and built a dashboard to streamline 
HR reporting.

## Dataset
- Source: Employee attendance records 
  (HR Analytics dataset)
- Contains daily presence data including work-from-home, 
  work-from-office, sick leave, and other leave types
- Covers a 3-month period across multiple departments

## Approach
1. Cleaned raw attendance data and standardised 
   leave type categories
2. Calculated presence %, WFH %, and sick leave % 
   by employee, team, and time period
3. Identified day-of-week patterns in WFH and 
   office attendance
4. Surfaced potential burnout signals through 
   sick leave clustering analysis
5. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for 
   HR team use

## Key Findings
- Monday and Friday had the highest WFH rates 
  (67% and 71% respectively), suggesting employees 
  prefer in-office mid-week
- Sick leave spiked 3x in the third month, 
  potentially indicating team fatigue or seasonal 
  illness — worth investigating at department level
- Employees with over 85% office presence had 
  20% higher sick leave rates than hybrid workers, 
  suggesting hybrid models may support better 
  wellbeing outcomes

## Business Recommendation
1. Formalise a Tuesday–Thursday in-office policy 
   aligned with natural employee preference patterns
2. Investigate the month-3 sick leave spike at 
   department level to identify if workload or 
   environmental factors are involved
3. Consider piloting a structured hybrid model 
   for high-presence teams showing elevated 
   sick leave rates

## Result
Dashboard reduced HR reporting time by 25% and 
enabled data-driven identification of work 
preference patterns and workplace improvement 
opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI (interactive dashboard)
- Microsoft Excel (data cleaning)
- SQL (attendance aggregation queries)
